# Privacy policy for Video Explorer

Effective: 11 November 2026

Video Explorer is a Chrome extension that lists the videos and GIFs on the page you're viewing so you can preview, copy and download them. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, and no server of its own. Nothing it reads is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What it reads

- **Website content.** When you open the popup, it reads the current page to find videos and GIFs: their links, titles, sizes and posters, and the page's own title and address.
- **Network responses.** In the background, it looks at the responses your browser receives, on every tab, to notice video and GIF files that players load by script. For those files it keeps the address, file type and size. It also notes when a site answers "too many requests", so it can slow down.

## Where it's kept

All of it stays in your browser:

- The list of videos found on each tab is kept in memory only. It's cleared when the tab goes to a new page or closes, and when the browser closes.
- File sizes, lengths and resolutions it has looked up are kept in memory until the browser closes, so reopening the popup doesn't ask the site again. You can turn this off or clear it on the settings page.
- Your view choices (layout, sorting, grouping, hidden formats) and settings are saved in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer.

## What it sends, and to whom

Video Explorer never sends data to the developer or to third parties. It only contacts the websites the videos come from, and only to:

- load thumbnails, sizes and lengths of the files it lists;
- download files you choose to download.

Those requests go straight from your browser to that website, with your browser's cookies for it, just as the page itself would load the file.

When you copy a download command, it's put on your clipboard, nowhere else. By default the command includes the page's address (as the Referer) and your browser's user agent, so the download works like it does in the browser. You can switch that off in the command menu. Cookies are never included.

## Limited use

Video Explorer uses the data above only to show and download the videos and GIFs on the page you're viewing. It doesn't sell or transfer user data, and doesn't use it for anything unrelated to that purpose, such as advertising or creditworthiness. Its use of information follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/), including the Limited Use requirements.
